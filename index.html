THE PROBLEM

Helping should be easier.

Many people want to participate, but face scattered opportunities, rigid schedules or unclear information. Organizations need reliable volunteers and better tools to manage them.

16% of Peru’s working-age population volunteered in 2023, according to the figure cited in the project.
⏱

Limited time

Long commitments conflict with study and work schedules.

🔎

Scattered information

Opportunities are often scattered across social networks, messages and separate channels.

🤝

Complex management

NGOs need easier ways to recruit, verify and follow up with volunteers.

A HUMAN DIFFERENCE

Helping also means taking care of yourself.

The project includes support features to prevent frustration, stress and emotional burnout, especially in sensitive activities.

01 Self-care workshops Preparation and tools for demanding experiences.
02 Emotional follow-up Spaces to identify needs and guide support.
03 Community and motivation Recognition, connection and positive feedback.
